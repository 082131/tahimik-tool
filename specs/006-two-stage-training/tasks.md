## Implementation Strategy

**MVP**: Phase 3 plus T009. Together they cover everything runnable today —
Stage 1 works, and skipping Stage 2 does not crash.

**Highest value**: **T013**. Until checkpoints carry a commit SHA, no training
result can be defended as reproducible, which undermines every number produced
downstream regardless of how good the model is.

**Cheapest with real payoff**: T015, a one-line fix preventing a silent
model/tokenizer mismatch the moment you switch to `byt5-base`.

## Notes

- T012 and T013 change behaviour and belong on their own branch, separate from
  any documentation change.
- T016 is deliberately phrased as an audit, not a fix. The outcome may be that
  all nine categories are already implemented.
