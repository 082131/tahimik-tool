## Phase 3: User Story 1 - Compress at a fixed rate during training (Priority: P1) 🎯 MVP

**Goal**: The gate learns to hit one fixed deletion target, identically for
every sentence.

**Independent Test**: `pytest tests/test_delete_gate.py -v`

### Tests for User Story 1 ⚠️

- [ ] T001 [US1] Confirm `tests/test_delete_gate.py` already covers gate-score bounds and rate range; cite the test names here rather than writing duplicates (SC-001)
- [ ] T002 [US1] Confirm `tests/test_model_forward.py::test_training_forward_runs_and_reports_a_valid_rate` covers the differentiability requirement via its `rate.grad_fn is not None` assertion (SC-002)
- [ ] T003 [US1] Write a noise-blindness test: the same batch with and without `noise_scores` must produce an identical deletion rate in `noise_adaptive=False` mode. No existing test appears to assert this, and it is the defining property of the baseline (FR-005)

### Implementation for User Story 1

- [ ] T004 [P] [US1] Verify the gate sits after `delete_gate_layer` and splits the encoder into pre- and post-gate halves (FR-001)
- [ ] T005 [P] [US1] Verify gate scores are `k · sigmoid(Linear(LayerNorm(H)))`, bounded in `[k, 0]` with `k = -30.0` (FR-002)
- [ ] T006 [US1] Verify soft deletion adds the gate score to the extended attention mask in log space and never multiplies the binary mask (FR-003) — the silent-failure case documented in `research.md`
- [ ] T007 [P] [US1] Verify the rate-loss target is a single shared constant with no noise conditioning (FR-005)
- [ ] T008 [P] [US1] Verify the variant emits its own `fixed_deletion_target` so the loss does not fall back to a hardcoded default (FR-006)
- [ ] T009 [P] [US1] Verify padding is excluded from both numerator and denominator of the deletion rate (FR-007)

**Checkpoint**: Fixed-rate training behaviour verified without a dataset.
