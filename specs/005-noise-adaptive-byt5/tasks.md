## Phase 4: User Story 2 - Gradient isolation holds under the full loss (Priority: P1)

**Goal**: `L_NE` trains only the estimator; nothing else reaches it, and it
reaches nothing else.

**Why equal priority to Story 1**: if this fails, the encoder is no longer a
control variable, the three-variant comparison is unfair, and Story 1's result
is unmeasurable regardless of how well the mechanism works.

## Implementation Strategy

**MVP**: Phases 3 and 4 together. Story 1 proves the mechanism adapts; Story 2
proves the comparison is fair. Neither alone is sufficient — an adaptive
mechanism measured unfairly proves nothing, and a fair comparison of a
non-adaptive mechanism proves nothing either.

**Highest value**: **T016**. It is the only item here describing a failure that
would corrupt results without any visible symptom. Everything else either
already works or is visibly incomplete.

**Do not start**: T017 and T018 until you have decided them.
